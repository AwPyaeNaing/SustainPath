The Commuter's Tax

Congestion acts as a "hidden tax" on citizens. It is not paid in cash, but in three distinct categories of loss that affect our quality of life.

01
Opportunity Cost
The loss of potential gain. Every hour spent in traffic is an hour stolen from education, work, or rest.
02
Physiological Stress
The link between traffic and high cortisol (stress) levels, which can lead to long-term health issues and fatigue.
03
Resource Inefficiency
Wasted energy. Vehicles consume fuel and release CO2 without moving, causing massive environmental harm for zero benefit.

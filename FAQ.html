Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is SustainPath? ⭐

SustainPath is a project focused on creating smarter and more sustainable city solutions, especially for traffic, parking, and environmental challenges.

2. What problem is SustainPath trying to solve? ⭐

We aim to reduce traffic congestion, pollution, wasted fuel, and inefficient parking systems in urban areas.

3. Why is sustainable transportation important? ⭐

Sustainable transportation helps lower air pollution, save energy, reduce traffic, and improve people's quality of life.

4. How can individuals help reduce traffic pollution? ⭐

People can use public transportation, carpool, walk, cycle, or support eco-friendly transportation systems.

5. Does SustainPath support environmental awareness? ⭐

Yes, one of our goals is to spread awareness about sustainability and environmental responsibility.
